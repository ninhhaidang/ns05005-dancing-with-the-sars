## The story in four steps

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="1 · Clouds hide the delta" icon="warning">
		In late October 2026, Sentinel-2 saw cloud-free ground over only about a third of the province. NISAR's L-band SAR works through cloud,
		day or night. We use the three NISAR passes that cover all of Hưng Yên: 22 Oct and 3 Nov (same descending track, 20 m) and 27 Oct (ascending, 10 m).
		[Data →](/data/nisar/)
	</Card>
	<Card title="2 · Something filled with water" icon="random">
		A statistical test on the 22 Oct → 3 Nov image pair, corrected for speckle, finds that 99.89% of the province did not change significantly.
		The largest change is a rectangular block of about 1.6 km² beside a river, where HH and HV both fell by about 6 dB.
		This is what a smooth water surface looks like to radar.
		[Swipe it on the map →](/map/?view=change)
	</Card>
	<Card title="3 · Land cover from radar only" icon="puzzle">
		1,347 hand-drawn label polygons and 12 SAR features (HH and HV on three dates) train a Random Forest that maps water, vegetation, bare soil and built-up land for the whole province, including the parts under cloud.
		[Land cover map →](/map/?view=landcover)
	</Card>
	<Card title="4 · How much to trust it" icon="approve-check">
		Eight experiments test models, features and validation schemes. Ten models agree on 65.9% of the area. 8.3% of the area lies outside the area of applicability. Built-up is the least reliable class.
		[Confidence map →](/map/?view=confidence) · [Limitations →](/limitations/)
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
